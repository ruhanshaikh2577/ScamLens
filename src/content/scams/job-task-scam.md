title: "Job task scam"
longTitle: 'Is this "like videos, earn daily" job a scam? Yes — how the task scam works'
description: "The job task scam explained: small rewards for likes, then deposit demands to unlock higher tiers. Warning signs, regional variants, and how to verify a real job."
tag: "Jobs"
intro: "A recruiter contacts you about an easy online part-time job: like videos, rate products, or complete small tasks for instant pay. The first few tasks really do pay a small amount. Then you must pay a deposit or recharge to unlock higher-paying tiers — and every payment you send disappears while new fees keep appearing."
looksLike:
  - "\"Part-time job, work from phone, earn $50–$100/day liking videos. First tasks paid instantly via crypto or transfer.\""
  - "\"To activate Level 2 tasks with 3x commission, deposit $100 as a refundable security amount.\""
  - "A Telegram or WhatsApp group showing members posting payment screenshots while an admin assigns daily task sets"
warningSigns:
  - "You are paid small amounts first with no interview, contract, or real company details."
  - "Higher earnings require you to pay a deposit, recharge, or unlock fee first."
  - "The job arrived via WhatsApp, Telegram, or text from an unknown recruiter, not a real hiring process."
  - "Withdrawals or commissions are blocked until you pay taxes, penalties, or the next tier fee."
whyItWorks: "The tiny early payouts feel like proof the job is real, so the deposit demand looks like a routine upgrade instead of theft. Each tier promises bigger returns, which keeps victims paying one more fee to recover what they already sent."
whatToDo:
  - "Stop paying immediately — no real job ever asks workers to deposit money to earn."
  - "Screenshot the chats, payment addresses, and transaction records, then block the recruiter."
  - "Report it to your national fraud reporting service with the payment details and phone numbers used."
verify: "Check the company on its official website typed yourself — never the link the recruiter sent. Real employers hire through official postings and interviews, and never pay wages through task-recharge tiers. Report fraud to the FTC at reportfraud.ftc.gov, the FBI at ic3.gov, or your national cybercrime helpline."
faqs:
  - q: "I really received money for the first tasks — can it still be a scam?"
    a: "Yes. Scammers pay tiny amounts first to build trust. The profit comes later, when you pay deposits and tier fees that are never returned."
  - q: "What is the deposit or recharge they ask for?"
    a: "A fake requirement to unlock higher-paying tasks. Real jobs never ask employees to pay to work — any such demand is theft."
  - q: "I already paid a deposit — what should I do?"
    a: "Do not pay any release or penalty fee. Save all chats and receipts, block the contacts, tell your bank, and report to ic3.gov, reportfraud.ftc.gov, or your national cybercrime service."
