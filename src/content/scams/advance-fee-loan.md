title: "Advance-fee loan scam"
longTitle: 'Is this "instant loan, pay a fee first" offer a scam? Yes — how it works'
description: "The advance-fee loan scam explained: anyone approved instantly, then processing fees demanded upfront while contacts are harvested for extortion. Warning signs and safe checks."
tag: "Credit"
intro: "An ad or message promises an instant loan with no credit check — anyone approved in minutes. Before disbursal you must pay a processing, insurance, or GST fee, often more than once. Some apps also harvest your contacts, then threaten to message them if you question the fees or miss payments. Real lenders deduct fees from the loan or state them in a contract — they never demand wallet transfers before disbursal."
looksLike:
  - "\"Instant loan approved! Rs 50,000 credited after a Rs 2,500 processing fee. Pay now to release the amount.\""
  - "\"Payday loan guaranteed, bad credit OK — send $150 insurance fee first via gift card or transfer.\""
  - "A loan app asking for full access to contacts, photos, and SMS before showing any loan agreement"
warningSigns:
  - "Approval is instant with no credit check, income proof, or paperwork."
  - "You must pay fees upfront by transfer, gift card, or wallet before receiving anything."
  - "The app demands access to contacts and galleries unrelated to lending."
  - "New fees keep appearing — insurance, then tax, then clearance — every time you pay one."
whyItWorks: "Urgency for cash makes the upfront fee look like a small price for fast approval. Contact access gives scammers leverage: embarrassment and threats keep victims paying long after the loan never arrives."
whatToDo:
  - "Do not pay any upfront fee or grant contact access — delete the app and block the lender."
  - "If contacts were harvested, warn family and friends that threatening messages are from scammers."
  - "Report the app, numbers, and payment details to your national fraud reporting service."
verify: "Check the lender on your central bank or regulator registry typed yourself — real lenders are licensed and give written agreements before any fee. Never install loan APKs from links. Report to the FBI at ic3.gov, the FTC at reportfraud.ftc.gov, or your national cybercrime helpline."
faqs:
  - q: "Why do they ask for a processing fee first?"
    a: "That fee is the theft. Real lenders state fees in a signed agreement and deduct them from the disbursal — they never ask for wallet or gift-card payments before releasing a loan."
  - q: "The app wants my contacts — is that normal?"
    a: "No. Lending never needs your contact list or photo gallery. That permission exists so scammers can threaten and shame you later."
  - q: "They threaten to send my photos to my contacts — what do I do?"
    a: "Do not pay. Warn your contacts, save the threats as evidence, block the numbers, and report to your national cybercrime service immediately."
