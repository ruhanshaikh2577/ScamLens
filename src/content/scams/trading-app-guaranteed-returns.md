title: "Trading app guaranteed returns"
longTitle: 'Is this trading app with guaranteed returns a scam? Yes — how it works'
description: "The fake trading app scam explained: dashboards showing growing profits, withdrawal fees that never end, and disappearance. Warning signs and verification steps."
tag: "Investment"
intro: "A mentor, ad, or group invites you to a trading app showing steady guaranteed profits, often with celebrity endorsements or luxury screenshots. Small deposits appear to grow on the dashboard — but every withdrawal needs another deposit, tax, or fee first. The profits are fake numbers; the deposits are real and gone when the app disappears."
looksLike:
  - "\"VIP crypto group — mentor signals, 10% daily profit guaranteed. Download the app here and start with $200.\""
  - "A polished app dashboard showing your balance growing every day with winning-trade notifications"
  - "\"To withdraw, pay the 20% clearance fee / tax / VIP upgrade first — then funds release instantly.\""
warningSigns:
  - "Guaranteed, risk-free, or fixed daily returns — impossible in real markets."
  - "Recruitment through WhatsApp, Telegram, dating apps, or celebrity ads instead of a licensed broker."
  - "Small withdrawals work at first, then large ones are blocked pending fees or upgrades."
  - "The app is sideloaded from a link or APK rather than an official app store listing."
whyItWorks: "The rising dashboard feels like proof, and early small payouts — funded by newer victims — build trust right up to the big deposit. By the time withdrawal fees appear, sunk cost makes one more payment feel cheaper than admitting the loss."
whatToDo:
  - "Stop sending money immediately — every release fee or upgrade is the final squeeze."
  - "Screenshot the app, chats, payment addresses, and transaction IDs before they vanish."
  - "Tell your bank and report to your national fraud service with the evidence saved."
verify: "Check the broker on your securities regulator register typed yourself — licensed firms never recruit via chat groups or guarantee returns. Real brokers hold funds in accounts matching their registered name, never personal wallets. Report to the FBI at ic3.gov or your national fraud service."
faqs:
  - q: "Can any app guarantee daily trading profits?"
    a: "No. Markets have no guaranteed returns. Any app promising fixed daily percentages is showing simulated numbers you cannot withdraw."
  - q: "Why did my dashboard balance keep growing?"
    a: "Those numbers are fake, designed to make you deposit more. Withdrawals are blocked with fee excuses — that is the theft, not a real balance."
  - q: "How do I check a broker is legitimate?"
    a: "Search the regulator register yourself and confirm the broker's official domain. Licensed advisers never recruit through Telegram groups or demand crypto deposits to personal wallets."
