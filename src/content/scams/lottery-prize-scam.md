title: "Lottery / prize scam"
longTitle: 'Did I really win a lottery? How "you won" advance-fee prize scams work'
description: "Lottery scam explained: surprise win notifications demand taxes or fees via gift cards or wire before a phantom prize that never arrives."
tag: "Prizes"
region: "global"
intro: "A call, text, or letter says you won a lottery, sweepstakes, or prize draw you never entered — but you must pay taxes, customs, or processing fees first via gift cards, wire, or crypto. The prize does not exist; the fee is the theft. Variants include Publishers Clearing House calls plus gift cards (US), Royal Mail lottery letters (UK), 'você ganhou' messages demanding boleto taxa (Brazil), and KBC lottery WhatsApp messages (India)."
looksLike:
  - "'Congratulations — you won $2.5M in Publishers Clearing House. Pay $500 clearance via gift cards to release it.'"
  - "'Royal Mail: a lottery parcel is held — pay the release fee here' with a link that is not royalmail.com."
  - "'Você ganhou um prêmio! Pague a taxa via boleto/PIX para liberar' — plus pressure not to tell anyone."
warningSigns:
  - "You 'won' a draw you never entered, with a prize far larger than any ticket you hold."
  - "Advance fee required: taxes, customs, processing, or insurance before delivery."
  - "Payment demanded by gift card, wire, crypto, or boleto — real lotteries deduct tax, never ask for cards."
  - "Secrecy pressure and urgency ('claim within 24 hours or forfeit') plus a callback number, not an official office."
whyItWorks:
  "A surprise win triggers excitement that suppresses scepticism, and a small fee feels rational against a huge prize. Each payment is framed as the last step, so victims pay repeatedly — fees, then 'extra tax,' then 'customs' — chasing a delivery that never comes."
whatToDo:
  - "Do not pay any fee. Real wins never require gift cards, wire, or crypto to release a prize."
  - "Cut contact and keep the message, number, and payment details as screenshots for your report."
  - "Report: US reportfraud.ftc.gov, UK actionfraud.police.uk, India 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in."
verify:
  "Check the win only via the official lottery operator (typed address yourself) or the retailer where you bought a ticket. Real lotteries publish winners, never notify via random WhatsApp, and never collect 'tax' by gift card."
faqs:
  - q: "I never bought a ticket — can I still win?"
    a: "No. If you did not enter, you cannot win. Any surprise-win message demanding a fee is an advance-fee scam."
  - q: "Why do they ask for gift cards or wire?"
    a: "Those payments are irreversible and untraceable. Real lotteries deduct tax from winnings — they never ask winners to pay by card, wire, or crypto."
  - q: "I already paid a fee — what now?"
    a: "Stop paying, call your bank or card issuer, screenshot everything, and report at reportfraud.ftc.gov (US), actionfraud.police.uk (UK), or 1930 (India)."
